Splash options

A better splash

Opening the app shows three screens in a row: the system launch screen (one static image on one color, set in Info.plist), the in-app LaunchView while the session restores, and then Welcome or Home. A better splash is mostly about making those three feel like one moment. Each option below shows the launch screen, the restoring state (animated), and the full sequence, which you can replay.

TodayTwo jumps in half a second

The background switches from flat color to gradient when LaunchView appears. The wordmark moves up about 24pt when the spinner joins the stack, then another 27pt when Welcome lays it out above the buttons. On a fast restore these read as flickers, not motion.

Option AStill

Keep today's look and remove the jumps. The wordmark sits at Welcome's position from the first frame, so it never moves. The gradient fades in over the flat launch color, which reads as "lights coming up," especially in dark mode where the bloom rises. The spinner waits 0.5s before appearing, so most restores never show it.

Feel

Calm and native, like Apple's own apps. The same brand as today, but it looks finished.

Build

Pad the two wordmark PNGs. Give LaunchView Welcome's layout with the buttons hidden, and add a flat base under a fading themedBackground. Add a delayed spinner overlay.

Cost

Smallest. No new assets beyond re-exported PNGs. About 20 lines.

Risk

The padding offset assumes the button stack height is fixed. At AX sizes Welcome's wordmark moves, so the launch screen and Welcome will be a few points off there.

Option BSparkle Recommended

Option A's no-jump setup, plus three small sparkles around the wordmark: raspberry, purple, and a tiny one tucked into "Glitter." They twinkle during restore instead of a spinner and settle to still on Welcome. It gives the name a small wink without turning the app into a craft store.

Feel

Warm and a little playful. It's the only option where "Glitter" shows up in the design, not just the name.

Build

Everything in A, plus the SF Symbol sparkle ×3 with .symbolEffect(.breathe), which is iOS 18, so no availability guard. Re-export the PNGs with the sparkles.

Cost

Small. A plus about 25 lines and two PNGs.

Risk

Sparkle placement is tuned to Caveat's letterforms at 64pt. Attach them to the wordmark view instead of the screen so they scale with Dynamic Type.

Option B · iPadSparkle on iPad

These use the same wordmark and sparkles as iPhone. The system launch screen centers its image, so iPad gets its own padded PNG (asset catalog ipad idiom) that puts the wordmark on Welcome's spot, 64pt above center. The sparkles are placed relative to the wordmark, so they follow it into landscape without extra layout.

Scale

At 64pt the wordmark is small on a 13" iPad. We could size it up on regular width, but Welcome would need the same change, or it will jump again.

Assets

Still two launch PNGs (light and dark). Welcome's wordmark sits 70pt above the safe-area center on every iPhone and iPad in both orientations, so one padded image fits all of them.

Check

In the Sep 7 iPad Welcome screenshot, the "d" in "Organized" is clipped at its right edge, where sparkle 1 sits. Confirm the current build before placing sparkles.

Option CMonogram

The "OG" app icon tile, centered. When someone taps the icon, iOS zooms it into the launch screen, so the first frame continues that gesture. A soft glint sweeps across the tile during restore. Then the tile steps back and Welcome's wordmark fades in.

Feel

Very iOS, and it looks like a real app launch. It's compact and works the same on iPad.

Build

Export the icon art without the square's shadow as a launch PNG. LaunchView draws the tile with a masked LinearGradient glint.

Cost

Small to medium.

Risk

The app shows two brand marks in two seconds. The pink tile on navy in dark mode is the brightest thing in the app.

Option DDrill canvas

A tiny diamond-painting canvas: printed symbol grid first, then drills pop into place to make a heart while the session restores. Pure delight for diamond painters, and it's the most distinctive option.

Feel

Crafty and charming, and the most "this app".

Build

A Grid of 46 drills with staggered .transitions, plus a canvas launch PNG. Needs a Reduce Motion path.

Cost

Medium to large for something most people see for less than a second.

Risk

It's diamond-only, so coloring book users get no nod. A filling grid looks like a progress bar, but restore time is unknown. The wordmark also shrinks and then swaps to full size.
