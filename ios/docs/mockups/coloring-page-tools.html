Coloring page tools

Four things the web app can do on a coloring page that iOS can't yet: choose mediums, manage photos, reveal mystery pages, and keep color codes and swatches. Each one has two or three options. Pick one per section, or mix and match. My picks are marked.

Page dates aren't here. They already use the same date rows as diamond projects.

Short versionMy picks

ToolPickWhy
MediumsA · Chips and pickerYou can see what you used at a glance. The picker is the tag picker we already have, so you can add a new medium the same way you add a tag.
PhotosA · Long press, plus the viewerThis is how Photos and Files work. It adds no buttons to the page, and the same two actions are on the full-screen viewer for people who never long press.
MysteryA · Reveal cardIt's hard to miss when a page is still a mystery, and it goes away once you name it. Hiding it again is in the title menu.
ColorsB · Its own screenSwatch sheets are something you look at while you color. They get a full screen, and the page itself stays short.

Where it all goesThe page, top to bottom

This is the page with my picks in place. Mediums go right under the status because they're part of what the page is. Colors is a single row that opens its own screen. Notes and dates stay where they are today.

1 · INT-1214Mediums

You pick which mediums you used from your Mediums list, and you can add a new one without leaving the page. Every change saves right away. It needs a connection for now.

2 · INT-1216Photos

Two new actions: delete a photo, and choose which one is the main photo. The main photo is the one on the page and in the Library, and it's always the first in the list. Delete always asks first.

3 · INT-1217Mystery pages

In a mystery book, a page stays a mystery until you say what it turned out to be. Until then, the Library shows the page number instead of the photo, so nothing gets spoiled. You can reveal a page, change the answer, or hide it again.

4 · INT-1218Color codes and swatches

The same as the web: one free-text note for color codes, and up to 100 swatch photos. Swatch photos are private, like other uploads. Adding or removing them needs a connection.

After you pickWhat happens next

Build order

Mediums, then photos, then mystery, then colors. Each one is its own PR with screenshots.

Offline

The reveal saves offline, like status does. Mediums, photo deletes and swatches need a connection for now. The offline sync doesn't cover them yet.

Backend

I don't expect backend changes. Colors uses the web's existing color reference records, and Sol will check first that the app can reach them.
