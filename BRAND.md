release or that it is endorsed by Organized Glitter or its owner.

## What you may not do

Without prior written permission, you may not:

- Release another application or service under the Organized Glitter name.
- Use an Organized Glitter logo or app icon as your own product identity.
- Use the official artwork to market or brand a fork or competing product.
- Imply sponsorship, endorsement, or an official relationship.
- Register confusingly similar names, domains, social-media accounts, or app
  identifiers.

If you distribute a fork or substantially modified version, give it a distinct
name and replace the official logo, app icon, and other branded artwork.
